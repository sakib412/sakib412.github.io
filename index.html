About

Engineer first, leader by necessity.

I'm a full-stack engineer from Sylhet, Bangladesh 🇧🇩 who ended up running engineering at EzyCourse (by AppifyLab) — an all-in-one platform where creators build courses, communities, and entire businesses on their own branded sites and apps.

My day-to-day spans a large TypeScript monorepo: Next.js frontends, AdonisJS microservices, multi-tenant architecture, payments, real-time chat, and a drag-and-drop website builder. I care a lot about performance and cost-aware engineering — the kind where you do the query math before you ship.

Still a builder at heart: I write code every day, review most of what ships, and obsess over developer experience for my team.
